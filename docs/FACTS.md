# FACTS — Step 0 research (RASAD, SIH26251)

Researched: 5 Oct 2026. Every fact has a source and a confidence level.

**Confidence levels**
- **High** — official/government source, or several reputable outlets agree.
- **Medium** — one reputable outlet, a retired-officer quote, or a secondary compilation whose original link is listed but was not re-opened by us.
- **Low** — blog, forum, very old figure, or only seen in a search summary.

Anything we use in the model that is *not* backed here is a `PLACEHOLDER` (see `docs/PLACEHOLDERS.md`, created in Step 2).

**How RASAD stays different:**
1. **Closure as probability** — an 80%-confidence window from ~35 winters of real weather, for **both** axes (Zoji La *and* Manali–Leh) plus forward tracks. Not one fitted rule, and not Zoji La only.
2. **Drones as a real mode**, with payload by RFI altitude band, plus the **drone basing advisor** (nobody has this).
3. **Mixed-fleet assignment** (which mode carries which load) with a plain reason for every line. Not truck routing.
4. **Live adapter layer** with health, last sync and record count. Not a static list of sources.
5. We do **not** copy their what-if presets or field-app design. Our what-ifs are the four in the brief, each shown as before vs after.
