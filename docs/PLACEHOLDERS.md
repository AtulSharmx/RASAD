## Everything simulated for the prototype

- All forward posts, roadheads, cols and nodes (names, coordinates, altitudes, troop strength). Only Leh, Kargil, Drass and the real passes are real places.
- Stock levels, daily consumption history and the injected anomalies.
- IoT fuel-sensor readings, the road/track status bulletin and the helicopter allotment.
- Weather **after the last real day** (copied from a past winter). Weather up to that day is real (Open-Meteo).
